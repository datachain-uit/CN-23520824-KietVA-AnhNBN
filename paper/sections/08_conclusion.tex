In pyKT, multi-KC questions are trained as one token per KC with the question's label but evaluated from the history before the question, so a model can learn to copy the label. Pre-Question Conditioning states what a KC prediction may read: no label of its own question, and the same context as at evaluation. On XES3G5M it raises AUC by $+0.0050$ on DKT and $+0.0043$ on simpleKT, lowers ECE, and gives $+0.032$ and $+0.024$ on multi-KC questions, while the implementation matters little (at most 0.0006). The evidence for copying is direct on DKT and indirect on simpleKT, where the segment convention also costs AUC on immediate repeats. Future work: exact question boundaries for attention backbones (to test whether they remove this loss), other datasets and models, and question-level validation.
